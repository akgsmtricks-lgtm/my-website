Our Capabilities

Core Engineering Services

From optical reverse engineering to full multi-axis robotic automation and finite element structural simulation.

⚙️

1. Mechanical Design Engineering

Complete machine architecture, structural frame design, kinematic links, and mechanism engineering tailored for industrial scale and reliability.

• Deliverables: Assembly GA drawings, kinematic timing charts, component calculations.
📐

2. 2D Drafting & Detailing

Shop floor-ready fabrication drawings, title blocks adhering to ISO 7200, datum schemes, fit classes (H7/p6, H7/g6), and comprehensive bills of materials (BOM).

• Standards: ASME Y14.5M, ISO 1101, IS 2102 Medium Tolerances.
🖥️

3. 3D CAD Modeling

Parametric feature tree parts, large assemblies exceeding 10,000 components, weldment structural frames, and sheet metal unfolds.

• Native Software: SolidWorks, CATIA V5, Siemens NX, PTC Creo.
💡

4. Product Design & Development

From initial ergonomics and industrial styling to pilot production, tooling validation, and enclosure integration.

• Workflow: Concept Sketches → 3D Solid → FEA Stress Sim → 3D Printed Prototyping.
📡

5. Reverse Engineering

High-accuracy scan-to-CAD workflows. Conversion of raw STL/OBJ point clouds into native parametric SolidWorks, STEP, and IGES solids.

• Accuracy: ±0.010 mm with complete color deviation verification heatmaps.
🔬

6. FEA & Simulation

Static, dynamic, thermal, and modal analysis to identify stress concentrations, minimize mass, and ensure safety factor compliance.

• Scope: Von Mises stress, displacement, factor of safety, and frequency harmonic response.
🤖

7. Industrial Automation Systems

Turnkey Cartesian pick-and-place gantries, motorized indexing tables, pneumatic transfer links, and automated material handling systems.

• Components: Servo linear actuators, pneumatic rotary actuators, safety interlocks.
⚡

8. CAD Automation

Custom API macros, SolidWorks/CATIA automation scripts, design tables, and automated drawing sheet generation.

• Efficiency: Up to 70% reduction in repetitive parametric drafting tasks.
