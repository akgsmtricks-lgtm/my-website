Our Capabilities

End-to-End Mechanical Services

1. Mechanical Design Engineering

Full machine architecture, structural frame design, kinematic links, and mechanism engineering tailored for industrial scale and reliability.

2. 2D Drafting & Detailing

Shop floor-ready fabrication drawings, title blocks adhering to ISO 7200, datum schemes, fit classes, and comprehensive bills of materials (BOM).

3. 3D CAD Modeling

Parametric feature tree parts, large assemblies exceeding 10,000 components, weldment structural frames, and sheet metal unfolds.

4. Product Design & Development

From initial ergonomics and industrial styling to pilot production, tooling validation, and packaging integration.

5. Reverse Engineering

High-accuracy scan-to-CAD workflows. Conversion of raw STL/OBJ point clouds into native parametric SolidWorks, STEP, and IGES solids.

6. FEA & Simulation

Static, dynamic, thermal, and modal analysis to identify stress concentrations, minimize mass, and ensure safety factor compliance.

7. Manufacturing Engineering

CNC programming, CAM toolpaths, bending sequences, welding schedules, and assembly line balancing.

8. CAD Automation

Custom API macros, SolidWorks/CATIA automation scripts, design tables, and automated drawing sheet generation.
